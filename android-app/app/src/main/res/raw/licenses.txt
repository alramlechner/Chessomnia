Wordmark
--------
The "CHESSOMNIA" wordmark (res/drawable/logo_wordmark.xml) is set in the
Montserrat typeface -- Julieta Ulanovsky and contributors, licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1.
https://github.com/JulietaUla/Montserrat

Generated by tools/generate_wordmark.py. Only the letter outlines go into the
app; the font file itself is not redistributed. The E is deliberately set
without its vertical stem.

Under OFL 1.1, documents created with a font are not themselves subject to the
OFL.


App icon
--------
The king and queen silhouettes of the app icon and the mark shown on the home
screen (res/drawable/ic_launcher_*.xml, res/drawable/logo_mark.xml) are original
work, generated by tools/generate_app_icon.py, and are licensed under the Apache
License 2.0. They are not derived from the Cburnett board set listed above.
